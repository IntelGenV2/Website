The real machines on the shelf — Commodore, Apple, classic Mac, and a XP-era PC that still boots.

OVERVIEW

Retro Computers is the collection of vintage systems that sit behind the IntelGen lab aesthetic. Not emulators first — physical boxes: 8-bit micros, 16-bit home computers, beige Macs, a translucent G3, and a Windows XP Dell.

These machines are the reference point for boots, CRTs, and the projects that grow out of them.

COMMODORE

  • Commodore 64 — 8-bit flagship home computer
  • VIC-20 — earlier Commodore micro, the smaller sibling
  • Amiga 500 — 16-bit multimedia classic

APPLE
