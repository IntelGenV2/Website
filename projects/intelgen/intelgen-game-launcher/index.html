IntelGen Game Launcher

Software

See the games installed on your PC in one place. Click a cover to launch that copy through its usual store.

Download for Windows Windows · x64 setup

One shelf for your games

Select Rescan to find games you’ve installed through Steam, Epic, GOG or Xbox. You can scan without signing in to those stores. If you installed a game yourself, drop its EXE onto the library. Own the same game on two stores? Link the copies and choose which one to launch.

Groups and pins

Pin the games you keep coming back to, or put a few into a group. Close the group and its covers stack together. Open it and each game keeps the group’s border colour. If you uninstall a game, your playtime is still there when you reinstall it.

Big Picture

Open Big Picture to browse your library from the couch. Press left or right on your controller to spin through the covers. Press up to open a group or see game info, A to launch the selected game, and B to return to the side menu. You can use your mouse and keyboard in desktop mode.

The launcher keeps seven rotating daily backups while it’s open. You can also export a copy of your library.
