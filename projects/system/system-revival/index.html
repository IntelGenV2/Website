SYS-01

System Revival

Game

I want to make a game where you can walk around inside a broken computer. The motherboard is the level. You play the little bot trying to get it running again.

Walking around inside a PC

I started with a 90s PC and a pencil sketch. What happens when you fix the video hardware while you’re standing inside the machine? I want you to actually see the picture improve. Same idea with the sound card. You should be able to hear what you just fixed.

The SR-8 board

By Test 3 I was working out the machine chip by chip. In revision four of the SR-8 you can click a pin to see where that connection goes. Keeping the other lines hidden makes it possible to follow one fault across the board.

Repairs earn points to unlock parts. For the final boss, what matters is how much of the machine you actually have working. I’m still figuring out the ending.

Making a board I can take apart

My motherboard maker starts with a C64-style longboard. I can lift a chip and leave its socket behind, then fit something else there later. The starter has 97 parts fitted and 69 empty positions. Even the power supply has individual parts, so I can leave a whole voltage rail empty until a chip needs it.
