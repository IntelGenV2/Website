DIR // ABOUT

What this is

IntelGen is a lab label for builds from the bench — DIY computers, games, and experiments that lean into retro hardware and old-machine aesthetics.

Some projects live in solder and VGA; others live in browsers, Unity, or Electron. One of them is a CRT-styled command dashboard. The rest span BASIC mini-PCs, ternary OS playgrounds, PC fabricator games, and arena fighters.

The look of this site — BIOS boot, scanlines, phosphor glow — is the same language those machines speak.
