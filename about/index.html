About Me

I’m IntelGenV2. I collect old computers and make things for them. I use the Studio 256 intro on several of my Commodore 64 games.

What I make

I wanted to play Bloxorz on the C64, so I ported all 33 stages, passcodes and all. Loophole is another one of my C64 games. You get a room, a button and a door. The trick to getting out changes forty times.

On the hardware side, I built NanoLab 2 with two Arduino Nanos. One runs BASIC and the other handles the VGA display. I can plug in a keyboard, write a program and save it to a microSD card.

I’ve also got a collection of machines, including a VIC-20, an Amiga 500 and a Macintosh Plus. The Apple IIe is still my pick of the Apple IIs.
