About Me

I’m IntelGenV2. I collect old computers. The Commodore 64 is the one I make games and tools for. I use the Studio 256 name for every project I release.

Making C64 games

I want to make games, so I started by looking for ones simple enough to port to the C64. Gun Mayhem 2 was my first game for the platform. My port is called Gun Mayhem 64.

I wanted to play Bloxorz on the C64, so I ported all 33 stages, passcodes and all. Loophole is another one of my C64 games. You get a room, a button, and a door. The trick to getting out changes forty times.

NanoLab

On the hardware side, I built NanoLab 1 with two classic Arduino Nanos (AVR). For NanoLab 2, I switched to two Nano R4s (ARM). I upgraded the display board for better graphics. On both machines, one board runs BASIC and the other drives the VGA display. I type programs on a PS/2 keyboard and save them to EEPROM.

My collection

Alongside my Commodore 64, I’ve got a VIC-20, an Amiga 500, a Macintosh Plus, and an Apple IIe. You can see the rest on my Retro Computers page.
